A PRACTICAL TRAIL CHECKLIST

Look after yourself out there.

Use this as a starting point, then confirm your route and conditions with a qualified local guide.

01 · ALTITUDE

Go up gradually

Build acclimatization and rest days into high-altitude routes. If you feel unwell, stop ascending and tell your guide; do not continue higher while symptoms are present.

02 · WARNING SIGNS

Act early when symptoms change

Headache, nausea, dizziness, unusual tiredness, or loss of appetite can be signs of altitude illness. Confusion, poor coordination, breathlessness at rest, or worsening symptoms need urgent help and descent with assistance.

03 · BEFORE EACH WALK

Check the plan and conditions

Review weather, route, permits, guide requirements, and a daily check-in plan. Carry layers, rain protection, a headlamp, water, a small first-aid kit, and insurance that covers high-altitude evacuation.

If you or a trekking partner becomes seriously unwell: tell your guide, seek urgent local medical help, and follow professional advice. Do not rely on this website for diagnosis or treatment.

Before you go.

Is Him-Path a real trekking agency?

Him-Path is a college project prototype. Route and price information is sample content; confirm details with registered local providers.

Are displayed prices in Nepalese rupees?

Yes. Quotes are in Nepalese rupees. This project demo records your selected payment method as pending; it does not charge money.

Can I rent equipment?

Choose rental dates, enter your contact details and select a payment method. No account is required; payment remains pending until availability is confirmed.
